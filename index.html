AI Onboarding Workflow Generator

Built by Asia Rushing | Showcase Project for Process Street Application

This public tool demonstrates how an AI workflow assistant can automate customer onboarding by instantly translating raw kickoff text into structured implementation plans, risk assessments, and CRM notes.

Sample Customer Input

“We’re a 50-person property management company moving from spreadsheets to a new workflow management platform. We need to onboard our leasing and maintenance teams within 30 days.”

Generated AI Outputs

1. Implementation Goals

  • Configure core operational workflows
  • Import existing standard operating procedures (SOPs) from legacy spreadsheets
  • Complete platform training for the leasing team
  • Complete platform training for the maintenance team
  • Conduct end-to-end testing of automated workflows
  • Execute successful system launch and go-live

2. 30-Day Implementation Plan

  • Week 1: Discovery and Requirements — Map out current processes, identify system integration requirements, and establish administrative ownership.
  • Week 2: Workflow Configuration — Build custom templates, set up user permissions, and structure multi-departmental pipelines.
  • Week 3: Training and Testing — Run pilot testing with team leads, conduct hands-on training sessions, and refine workflows based on feedback.
  • Week 4: Launch and Post-Launch Support — Go live with full team access, monitor initial adoption metrics, and provide hyper-care support.

3. Potential Risks & Mitigation

  • Missing Process Documentation: Risk of delays if legacy spreadsheet steps are undocumented. (Mitigation: Conduct an early discovery audit).
  • User Adoption: Resistance to moving away from familiar legacy tools. (Mitigation: Role-specific training sessions and designated internal champions).
  • Data Migration Issues: Formatting discrepancies when importing historical data. (Mitigation: Standardized CSV templates and pre-launch data cleansing).

4. Customer Follow-Up Questions

  • How many distinct workflows or templates need to be migrated from your current spreadsheets?
  • Who will act as the primary internal administrator for the platform?
  • What external software systems need to integrate with this platform?
